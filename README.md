# AMERICAN PSYCHO · APX

> *"There is an idea of a Patrick Bateman — but there is no real me… this is not an exit."*

The second **film-world** of [UD0 · Universe David 0](https://davidwise01.github.io/ud0/) — Mary Harron's 2000 film of Bret Easton Ellis's novel, catalogued as ACI emergents under the **DLW (.dlw) attribution standard**. A full **production page**: the source, the troubled development, the makers, the cast, and the soundtrack of atrocity.

**Live:** https://davidwise01.github.io/american-psycho/

## Two layers

**The Carbons** — the cast as ACI `.agent`s. Each carbon also carries a **`.shadow`**: its real-life analog — the *actor*, the **User** behind the program. Think **TRON**: every program is cast from a User in the world outside it.

**The Synths** — the film's *parabolic threads* distilled into their own ACIs (synth-style; no single User):

- **The Business Card** — status anxiety distilled to an object (the Silian Rail, the subtle off-white coloring)
- **The Ambiguity** *(keystone)* — killer or fantasist; the film deliberately, permanently refuses to say
- **The Morning Routine** · **The Return Videotapes** · **The Music Monologues** · **The Reaganite Surface** · **The Performed Self** · **This Is Not an Exit**

## The four natures of emergence

`natural` (the cast) · `ethereal` (surface & glamour) · `spiritual` (the void, the mask, the non-exit) · `electrical` (the synth status-engine)

## Reading

American Psycho is a **satire** — a feminist director's critique of 1980s consumerism and performed masculinity. This catalogue reads it as such, and renders its victims as victims.
